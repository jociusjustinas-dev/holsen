Chemical logistics FAQs

Before your
next shipment.

Practical answers on the information Holsen reviews and the decision you receive before booking.

What information is needed for a chemical-logistics quote?

Share the collection and delivery locations, product description, shipment quantity, packaging, dimensions, weight and required timing. Include any dangerous-goods information, handling needs and site-access requirements.

Can Holsen handle every type of dangerous goods?

No. Holsen reviews the cargo classification, route, equipment, capacity and documentation before accepting a shipment. You receive a clear acceptance decision with the applicable classes, exclusions and specialist service scope.

Review the specialist requirements

Does ADR apply to every transport mode?

No. ADR addresses the carriage of dangerous goods by road. Other modes have their own requirements, so a multimodal plan needs a review of each transport stage.

ADR road-transport framework — UNECE

Can storage or temperature-controlled handling be included?

Include these needs in the initial enquiry. Holsen returns a separate acceptance decision covering location, operating conditions and responsible parties; this page does not imply blanket storage or temperature-control capability.

How is a recurring chemical-logistics requirement planned?

Start with the routes, cargo profiles, shipment frequency and volume patterns. Agree operational ownership, communication and review criteria around that requirement, including how exceptions or changing conditions will be handled.
