Which industries does Holsen focus on?

Agriculture, Chemicals, Industrial, Consumer Goods, Paper & Packaging, Technology, Automotive, and Life Sciences & Healthcare. Holsen starts with the cargo and operating requirements, then returns a defined service scope for the proposed operation.

Explore industry requirements

What if our cargo does not fit one industry?

Describe the product, origin, destination, volume and timing. Include handling and documentation needs. Those requirements are more useful for planning than choosing a single industry label.

Review the planning requirements

Does an industry listing confirm specialist capability?

No. Holsen reviews dangerous-goods classification, controlled conditions, specialist handling and required certifications against the cargo, route and operating setup. You receive a clear acceptance decision and service scope before booking.

Review chemical cargo requirements

Do we need to choose a transport mode first?

No. Start with the cargo, route and delivery requirements. Holsen compares road, rail, sea, air and combined options against the complete operation before recommending a setup.

Compare transport modes

Can we discuss an ongoing logistics requirement?

Yes. Holsen structures recurring requirements around routes, expected volumes, seasonal changes and service needs. The proposed setup defines operational ownership, communication, onboarding and the measures used to review performance.
