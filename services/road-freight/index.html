Road freight FAQs

Before your
next shipment.

Practical answers for planning an international road-freight requirement.

What information is needed for a road-freight quote?

Provide the collection and delivery locations, cargo description, dimensions, weight, timing and shipment frequency. Add site-access, documentation and handling requirements; Holsen reviews them as one operation before returning the proposed scope and price.

How do I choose between direct transport and groupage?

Holsen compares shipment size, delivery windows, intermediate handling, route and cargo before recommending direct transport or groupage. The recommendation explains the operating trade-off, not only the mode label.

Explore the transport options

Can a recurring transport requirement be discussed?

Yes. Holsen structures recurring requirements around lanes, expected frequency, volume patterns and service needs. The proposed setup defines operational ownership and the measures used to review performance.

What if the cargo needs specialist handling?

Describe the cargo and its handling requirements at the start. Holsen reviews the required equipment, documentation, capacity and route before returning a clear acceptance decision and transport scope.

How is shipment visibility agreed?

Holsen assigns operational ownership for status updates and exception communication. Before transport begins, the setup records what your team receives, who receives it and who acts when conditions change.
