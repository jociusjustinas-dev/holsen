Services FAQs

Before choosing
your solution.

Start with the requirement. The transport mode is one part of the decision.

Which transport services does Holsen provide?

Holsen provides road, rail, sea and air freight, supported by contract logistics, customs and value-added services. The team proposes a multimodal plan when a single mode does not meet the route, capacity, timing or cargo requirements.

Explore the services

What if I do not know which mode to choose?

Share the cargo, origin, destination, volume and required timing. These define the options to assess; you do not need to choose a transport mode before starting the conversation.

Compare transport modes

Can one solution combine several transport modes?

Yes. Holsen compares the complete journey and connects the required modes in one proposed plan. The plan identifies the connections, transfers and responsible parties across the operation.

What information is needed for a quote?

Provide the collection and delivery locations, cargo description, dimensions, weight, timing and shipment frequency. Add documentation, access and handling requirements; Holsen reviews them as one operation before returning the proposed scope and price.

Can we discuss a recurring logistics requirement?

Yes. Holsen structures recurring requirements around routes, volume patterns and service needs. The proposed setup defines operational ownership, communication and the measures used to review performance.
