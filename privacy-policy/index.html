Before data is collected.

This draft defines the information that must be confirmed before any website form is connected to a recipient or storage system.

Client validation required

Not ready for publication.

The legal entity responsible for the website must approve the controller details, lawful bases, recipients, retention periods and rights-contact process below.

Information collected through forms

Depending on the form, the website may collect a business contact name, company, work email, optional phone number, enquiry details, shipment information, partner capacity information and user-selected attachments. The ESG report form collects a work email address.

Only information needed to assess and answer the specific request should be provided.

Purpose and legal basis

Enquiry data is intended to be used to assess and respond to the request. Before launch, Holsen must document and publish the applicable lawful basis for each form and any follow-up communication.

Recipients, storage and transfers

Before launch, Holsen must identify the approved recipients and processors, where the data is stored, whether it is transferred outside the EEA, the safeguards used for any transfer and a defined retention period for each form.

Your data-protection rights

The final policy must explain how people can exercise the rights that apply to their processing, how to contact Holsen about personal data and how to complain to the competent supervisory authority.

Required before launch

Details to confirm.

  • Controller’s approved legal name, registration details and address.
  • Privacy contact and data-protection officer details, where applicable.
  • Purpose and lawful basis for every form and any later marketing.
  • Processors, recipients, international transfers and safeguards.
  • Retention period or objective retention criteria for each data set.
  • How rights requests, complaints and security incidents are handled.
  • Any automated decision-making, profiling, analytics or cookies used.
